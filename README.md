### 👋 Hi, I'm Tomás

I'm a **Business Analyst and Product Owner at GFT Technologies Brasil**, working in financial services: capitalization, insurance, private pension and banking. I sit between business, regulation and engineering. I turn rules into clear specs and a sharp backlog, keep functional rules apart from technical design, and make sure squads in Java, Angular, AWS and Mainframe COBOL know exactly what to build.

I came to technology from teaching. Five years explaining dense ideas to very different audiences turned into what I do now: translating what the business needs into what engineers can deliver.

- 🔭 **Now:** regulatory and digital transformation demands for a capitalization company, such as Núclea's Resolution 12, with a squad of Java and Mainframe COBOL developers
- 🎓 **Studying:** MBA in Artificial Intelligence for Business, Faculdade Senac DF (2026–2027)
- 🧭 **How I work:** clarity before code · business rules apart from technical design · modern systems meeting legacy · AI-assisted, human-checked
- 🌐 **Portfolio:** cases, a sample user story and my CV at **[t0masleones.github.io](https://t0masleones.github.io)**
